# V11.1 amendment: training hardware only

Recorded on 2026-09-14, before any V11.1 training or evaluation job ran.

## Why

V11 stopped at its preflight because the local RTX 4060 Ti exposed 7,406,092,288 free bytes, 110,100,480 bytes below the 7 GiB pre-load gate ([V11 results](V11-RESULTS.md)). The owner separately authorized a rented Modal GPU to complete the campaign. This amendment is that separate hardware decision.

## What changes

- QLoRA preflight, pilot training, full training, and fresh-process reload run on a Modal GPU instead of the local card, through `scripts/modal_sql_v11.py`.
- The script calls the existing `sql_agent.v11_training.preflight`, `train`, and `reload_check` functions unchanged. Their `root` is a Modal Volume laid out like the local `artifacts/` directory.
- Modal jobs are recorded in `artifacts/v11/compute/modal-ledger.json` (GPU type, charged seconds, estimated `cloud_gpu_cost_usd`), separate from the local lease ledger so its format is untouched. The cloud spend cap is **$15**; Modal's billing page is authoritative.
- Model verification on Modal uses the recorded `asset-manifest.json` with only its `.cache/huggingface/*` download-metadata entries removed; those files are machine-specific. Every weight, tokenizer, and config checksum is still verified.
- Modal's exposure events are copied back to `artifacts/v11/compute/modal-exposure.jsonl`.

## What does not change

- Model: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct` at revision `dcc04bb77301e6368aa24a609c6ea496b70503e8`. It is downloaded on Modal directly from Hugging Face and verified against the recorded `asset-manifest.json` checksums before use.
- Frozen data: train `aac84867…`, validation `d8eba72d…`, development `2fdd8978…` (hashes checked by `llm_evals.campaign.open_dataset`).
- Recipes A (2e-4) and B (1e-4), seed 20260923, NF4 double quantization, rank 8, alpha 16, one epoch, completion-only loss, and gradient accumulation 16.
- Every gate, including the 7 GiB free-memory, 7.6 GiB peak-allocation, 2.2-hour projection, and finite-loss checks, plus all pilot, development, and regression thresholds in `sql_agent/v11_selection.py`.
- Evaluation code, SQL execution, the read-only authorizer, AST validation, limits, the official scorer, and database byte-hash audits. Evaluation calls the unchanged `v11_evaluation.run`.
- The 1,033-case locked final stays unopened and needs separate approval.

## What leaves the machine

Only the frozen `train`, `validation`, and `development` JSONL files and their manifests: public BIRD-training questions, schema text, generated evidence, and reference SQL. The `sql-agent` source tree and the checksum-pinned llm-evals 0.4.0 wheel are also uploaded. Public BIRD databases are uploaded only for evaluation, as described below. No receipts, credentials, or private data are uploaded. Trained adapters (~80 MB) are downloaded back into `artifacts/v11/sql-agent/training/`.

## Evaluation location (decided 2026-09-14, before any evaluation)

The local GPU had 7.8 of 8 GB in use by desktop applications, so the owner chose to run evaluation on Modal as well (A10G). The `evaluate` function in `scripts/modal_sql_v11.py` calls the unchanged `sql_agent.v11_evaluation.run`. SQL still runs in the read-only worker subprocess, and database hashes are snapshotted before and checked after each run.

Frozen cases store Windows database paths. To avoid rewriting any dataset, and therefore changing its hash, the function creates a symlink in its working directory whose name is the exact frozen path string. That symlink points to the uploaded copy of the database.

This adds to what leaves the machine: the 7 public BIRD-training development databases (1.59 GB) and the checksum-pinned official `evaluation_ex.py` scorer. The 11 V5 holdout databases (4.47 GB) and the prepared regression inputs are uploaded only if the development gate permits the regression stage. Evaluation reports are pulled back into `artifacts/v11/sql-agent/eval/`, and gate decisions are frozen locally with the unchanged `v11_selection` functions.

## Training-side dataset fingerprint (recorded 2026-09-14, after the first Modal preflight)

The first Modal preflight (30.7 GPU-seconds, about $0.02) stopped before loading the model. `llm_evals.dataset.dataset_fingerprint` hashes the **contents** of every database a case references, and the training and validation databases (26.3 GB) were not uploaded. This was a correct fail-closed stop, not a training result.

Training reads only the frozen prompt messages. Instead of uploading those databases, every train, validation, and development database was hashed locally from the real files. Each role's fingerprint was confirmed against its frozen manifest, and the result is stored in `artifacts/v11/compute/modal-asset-digests.json`. The wrapper refuses to start unless all three roles matched locally.

For training jobs only, the wrapper uses those recorded digests in place of reading database bytes. Everything else in the fingerprint computation is unchanged, the manifest hash must still match exactly, and an unknown asset path fails closed. Evaluation jobs use the real uploaded databases and the unmodified fingerprint.

## Peak-memory gate amendment (owner decision 2026-09-14, after preflight attempt `modal-a100-2`)

**Measured before this amendment:** On the A100-40GB, preflight loaded the model with no offload and completed one training step on 8 examples. Loss was 0.351 (finite), step time 7.0 s, and the projected 1,200-case duration was about 1,050 s (gate: at most 2.2 h). Peak allocation was **10,312,202,240 bytes**, above the frozen 7.6 GiB gate, so the preflight raised before saving its adapter. That failure is recorded in `artifacts/v11/compute/modal-ledger.json` (65.1 GPU-seconds).

**Why the gate changes:** The 7.6 GiB limit existed so training would fit the local 8 GB RTX 4060 Ti. It checks hardware fit and says nothing about model quality. After training moved to rented hardware, the owner explicitly chose to replace it with a limit that checks fit on that hardware: peak allocation at most **36 GiB** on the A100-40GB. This change was made after seeing the measurement and is disclosed as such. The recorded 10.31 GB peak passes it.

**Unchanged:** All other preflight checks (CUDA-only placement, no offload, finite loss, projected duration) and every model-quality gate in `sql_agent/v11_selection.py`: pilot EX lift of at least 0.05 with completion at least 0.95; development adapter EX at least 0.55, lift of at least 0.08, completion at least 0.95; regression EX at least 0.60, +0.05 over V5, grouped CI above 0, completion at least 0.97, p95 under 20 s. No `sql_agent` source was modified. Fresh-process reload is verified on each pilot adapter by `reload_check`.

**Implication for the local claim:** The measurement independently confirms that this 7B QLoRA recipe cannot train within the local 8 GB card's memory, the original V11 stop reason.
